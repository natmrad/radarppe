# Molecule AR – VSEPR shapes
Scan a QR card → the molecule opens → tap "View in my room (AR)".
All files stay at the top level of the repo (no folders).
- index.html: printable QR cards (with quiz mode)
- viewer.html: the 3D / AR page
- molecules.json: molecule info
- *.glb: 3D models (Android and computers)
- *.usdz: 3D models for iPhone/iPad AR
